# Westbridge University Student Support Policy

Fictional rules for the Westbridge University simulation.

Students may contact Student Support through the Student Portal or at
support@westbridge.example. The service acknowledges routine requests within
two working days and gives an expected response date if further review is
needed. Urgent wellbeing requests are prioritised during staffed hours; this
service is not an emergency response service.

Support staff record the request date, category, assigned team, actions, and
resolution in the restricted support record. They share personal details only
with staff who need them to respond, or where disclosure is required to
address an immediate safety concern. Students may ask Student Support to
correct factual errors in their record.
