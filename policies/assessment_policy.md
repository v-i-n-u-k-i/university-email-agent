# Westbridge University Assessment Policy

Fictional rules for the Westbridge University simulation.

Assessment instructions state the due date, submission method, and whether a
task is individually assessed. Unless the unit outline says otherwise, work
submitted late is accepted for up to five calendar days with a deduction of
5% of the available mark per day, capped at 25%. After five days, the work is
recorded as not submitted. Tasks designated as mandatory pass requirements
may have different rules stated in their unit outline.

A student may request an extension through the Student Portal before the due
date. The Course Office records the request and decision. Suspected academic
integrity concerns are referred to the Academic Review Office; teaching staff
must not change a recorded result while a review is open.
