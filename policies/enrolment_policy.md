# Westbridge University Enrolment Policy

Fictional rules for the Westbridge University simulation.

Students may add or change units through the Student Portal until the end of
teaching week 2. Enrolment is confirmed only when prerequisites are met and a
place is available. A student may take up to four standard units per teaching
period; a higher load requires written approval from the Course Office.

Students are responsible for checking their confirmed unit list and timetable.
Requests received after the change deadline require Course Office review and
are not automatically approved. The Registrar records each confirmed unit,
its unit code, the effective date, and its enrolment status in the student
record.
