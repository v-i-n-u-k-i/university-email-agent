# Westbridge University Withdrawal Policy

Fictional policy for the Westbridge University simulation. Applies to the
current teaching period.

## Deadlines

- Submit by 5:00 p.m. on the Friday of teaching week 2 to leave a unit without
  an academic withdrawal notation.
- Submit by 5:00 p.m. on the Friday of teaching week 6 to leave with a `W`
  notation and no unit mark.
- After week 6, ordinary withdrawal is closed. The Exceptional Circumstances
  Panel may review a late request when serious, documented events prevented
  an earlier application.

## Eligibility

An active student may request withdrawal from one or more currently enrolled
units. A student withdrawing from every unit should state that they are also
requesting a break in study; this request does not itself approve a leave of
absence.

## When withdrawal is not permitted

Withdrawal is not available for a unit that is already completed or has a
published final result. A request after the week 6 deadline is not accepted
through the ordinary process. A student cannot withdraw from a unit in which
they are not currently enrolled. Only the Exceptional Circumstances Panel may
approve an exception to a deadline.

## Recording a request

Submit the official Student Portal form to the Registrar, listing the student
ID, unit code or codes, submission date, and a short reason category. The
Registrar records the received timestamp, requested units, decision, decision
date, and effective date in the student record, then sends confirmation to the
student's university email. An email conversation alone is not a recorded
withdrawal request.
